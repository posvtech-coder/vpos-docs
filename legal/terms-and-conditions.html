👥 User Roles

VPOS has different user roles with specific permissions:

1. Admin (Platform Administrator)

  • Full system access
  • Manages billing devices and subscription plans
  • Creates shopkeeper accounts, service agents, and other admins
  • Can enable or disable user accounts
  • Views reports across all shopkeepers
  • Access: Admin application only

2. Service Agent (Support Staff)

  • Provides technical support to shopkeepers
  • Can create shopkeeper accounts (if authorized)
  • Can update shopkeeper information
  • Cannot change subscription plans or enable/disable accounts
  • Access: Admin application only

3. Shopkeeper (Business Owner)

  • Manages their own business operations
  • Creates and manages branch locations
  • Creates manager accounts for branches
  • Manages inventory, products, and images
  • Views sales reports and transaction history
  • Configures billing device settings
  • Access: Admin and Billing applications

4. Manager (Branch Manager)

  • Manages specific branch(es) assigned by shopkeeper
  • Manages inventory for assigned branches
  • Views sales reports for assigned branches
  • Processes billing transactions
  • Cannot create/delete branches or modify subscriptions
  • Access: Admin (branch view) and Billing applications

🗑️ Account Deactivation & Data Retention

Account Deactivation

Deactivated accounts keep all data but cannot access the system. Only admins can deactivate accounts.

Account Deletion

When an admin schedules an account for deletion:

  • 15-day grace period: Account remains active with all data
  • During this time, admins can cancel the scheduled deletion
  • After 15 days, the account and all data are permanently deleted
  • Applies to shopkeeper, service agent, and manager accounts

Data Retention Periods

Data Type How Long Why
Active Account Data Indefinite (while active) To provide services
Deactivated Accounts 90 days Allow reactivation
Scheduled Deletions 15 days Allow cancellation
Sales & Billing Records Minimum 7 years Indian tax law compliance
Product Images (when disabled) 90 days Allow re-enabling feature
System Logs 90 days Troubleshooting and security
Need Custom Retention? Contact support@vposindia.com or call 090190 69884 to discuss custom retention periods for your business needs.
