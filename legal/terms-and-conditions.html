👥 User Roles & Access Levels

VPOS operates with a role-based access control system. Each user role has specific permissions and capabilities:

1. Admin (Platform Administrator)

  • Full system access — manages billing devices, subscription plans, and all user accounts
  • Can create and manage shopkeeper accounts, service agents, and other admins
  • Can register and assign billing devices to branches
  • Can enable or disable user accounts
  • Can view reports and analytics across all shopkeepers
  • Access: VPOS Admin application only

2. Service Agent (Support Staff)

  • Support and assistance role — helps shopkeepers with technical issues
  • Can create shopkeeper accounts (if granted permission by admin)
  • Can update shopkeeper profile information (excluding account status and customer type)
  • Can view shopkeeper data for support purposes
  • Cannot enable/disable accounts or change subscription plans
  • Access: VPOS Admin application only

3. Shopkeeper (Business Owner)

  • Business management — manages their own business operations
  • Can create and manage branch locations
  • Can create and manage manager accounts for their branches
  • Can manage inventory, categories, and product images
  • Can view sales reports and transaction history
  • Can configure billing device settings and features
  • Access: VPOS Admin application (management) and VPOS Billing applications

4. Manager (Branch Manager)

  • Branch-level operations — manages specific branch(es) assigned by shopkeeper
  • Can manage inventory for assigned branches
  • Can view sales reports for assigned branches
  • Can process billing transactions
  • Cannot create or delete branches
  • Cannot modify subscription or device settings
  • Access: VPOS Admin application (branch view) and VPOS Billing applications

🗑️ Account Deactivation & Data Retention

VPOS has clear policies for account deactivation and data retention to protect your business data while maintaining system integrity.

Account Deactivation

Accounts can be temporarily deactivated by admins. Deactivated accounts retain all data but cannot access the system.

Account Deletion (Scheduled)

When an admin schedules an account for deletion:

  • 15-day retention period: The account is marked for deletion but remains active with all data intact
  • During this period, admins can cancel the scheduled deletion if needed
  • After 15 days, the account is permanently deleted along with all associated data
  • This applies to shopkeeper accounts, service agents, and manager accounts

Data Retention Periods

Data Type Retention Period Purpose
Active Account Data Indefinite (while active) Operational use
Deactivated Account Data 90 days Allow reactivation period
Scheduled Deletion Accounts 15 days Cancellation grace period
Sales & Billing Records Minimum 6 years Tax compliance (GST Act 2017 & Income Tax Act 1961)
Product Images (when disabled) 0-365 days (configurable) Flexible cleanup
Audit Logs 90 days Security monitoring
Activity Logs 90 days System tracking
Deleted User Backups 90 days Recovery and audit trail

Inventory Image Deletion

When shopkeepers disable product images for a branch:

  • Admins/Service agents can schedule deletion with a configurable delay (0-365 days)
  • During the delay period, images are retained and can be restored by re-enabling the feature
  • After the scheduled date, images are permanently deleted by an automated system task
  • This feature provides flexibility for temporary disablement without immediate data loss

Transaction Cleanup

Old transaction records may be archived or deleted based on retention policies:

  • Minimum 3-year retention for tax compliance
  • Transactions older than the retention period may be archived or deleted
  • Automated cleanup tasks run daily to maintain system performance
Need Changes to Retention Periods? If you require custom retention periods for your business (for example, longer retention for auditing purposes or shorter cleanup cycles), please contact our support team at support@vposindia.com or call 090190 69884. We will work with you to configure appropriate retention settings.
