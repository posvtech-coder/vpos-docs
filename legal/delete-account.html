📝 Request Account Deletion

You can request the permanent deletion of your VPOS Admin account and associated data at any time. This page explains the deletion process, what data is removed, and what is retained for legal compliance.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Please export any data you wish to keep before submitting a deletion request.

How to Request Deletion

Follow these steps to request account deletion:

  1. Contact Support: Send an email to
  2. Use Subject Line: "VPOS Admin Account Deletion Request"
  3. Include Your Details:
    • Your registered phone number (used for login)
    • Your full name as registered in VPOS
    • Reason for deletion (optional but helpful)
  4. Confirmation: You will receive a confirmation email within 24-48 hours
  5. Processing Time: Account deletion is completed within 30 days of your request

🗂️ What Data Gets Deleted

When you request account deletion, the following data is permanently removed from our systems:

Data Type Details Deletion Timeline
Personal Information Name, email address, phone number, business address, user profile Within 30 days
Authentication Data Phone authentication credentials, Firebase Auth tokens, session data Immediate
Product Images All product photos uploaded to inventory Within 30 days
Inventory Data Product listings, categories, pricing, stock levels Within 30 days
Branch Information Branch profiles, manager assignments, device registrations Within 30 days
User Preferences App settings, notification preferences, UI customizations Within 30 days
Downloaded Files Excel/PDF reports saved to your device (under your control) Delete manually from device

⏳ What Data is Retained

Due to legal and business requirements, certain data must be retained even after account deletion:

Legal Requirement: Indian tax laws (GST Act, Income Tax Act) require businesses to retain financial transaction records for a minimum of 6 years. This retention applies to aggregated business data, not personal identifiable information.
Data Type Retention Period Reason
Anonymized Transaction Records 6 years GST and Income Tax compliance (India)
Crash Logs (Production Only) 90 days App stability and debugging (no personal identifiers)
Audit Logs 90 days Security monitoring and fraud prevention
Aggregated Analytics Indefinite Service improvement (fully anonymized, no personal data)

Important: Retained financial data is anonymized — your name, email, and phone number are removed. Only transaction amounts, dates, and business metrics are kept for tax compliance.

📦 Export Your Data Before Deletion

We recommend exporting your business data before requesting account deletion. You can export the following directly from the VPOS Admin app:

How to Export Data

  1. Sales Reports: Go to "Branch Reports" → Select date range → Download Excel or PDF
  2. Product Inventory: Go to "Inventory" → Use app's export feature (if available) or take screenshots
  3. Customer Lists: Contact support for a comprehensive data export (CSV format)
Tip: Downloaded Excel/PDF files are stored on your device and remain under your control even after account deletion. Make sure to back them up to cloud storage or external drives.

🔄 Partial Data Deletion (Without Account Deletion)

You can delete specific data types without closing your VPOS Admin account:

What You Can Delete In-App

  • Product Images: Delete individual inventory item photos from the Inventory screen
  • Inventory Items: Remove specific products from your catalogue
  • Branch Data: Delete branches and associated manager assignments (admin privilege required)
  • Downloaded Reports: Excel/PDF files are stored on your device — delete via file manager

What Requires Support Request

  • Phone Number Change: Contact support to update your primary login phone number
  • Email Update: Update directly in-app or contact support
  • Business Address Change: Update directly in your profile settings

Note: Deleting individual data items does not affect your account or subscription status.

⚠️ Important Considerations

Account Deletion is Permanent

  • You will lose access to all VPOS Admin features immediately after deletion is processed
  • Your subscription will be cancelled (no refunds for remaining time)
  • All linked billing devices will be unassigned and deactivated
  • Branch managers and staff will lose access to your business data

Grace Period

As per our Terms & Conditions, account deletion follows a 15-day grace period. During this time:

  • Your account is marked for deletion but remains accessible
  • You can cancel the deletion request by contacting support
  • After 15 days, deletion becomes final and irreversible

Alternative Options

Before deleting your account, consider these alternatives:

  • Pause Subscription: Contact support to temporarily suspend your account
  • Downgrade Plan: Switch to a lower-cost plan instead of deleting
  • Transfer Ownership: Transfer your account to another team member

🔒 Data Security During Deletion

We take data security seriously, even during the deletion process:

  • Secure Deletion: All personal data is permanently deleted using secure erasure methods
  • Encrypted Transit: All data transmitted during deletion requests is encrypted with TLS 1.2+
  • Verification Required: We verify your identity before processing deletion to prevent unauthorized requests
  • Audit Trail: Deletion actions are logged for 90 days (logs contain no personal data)
