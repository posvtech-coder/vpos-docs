📱 Billing Devices

Device Provisioning

Important: Billing devices are provided by Value Tech Solutions only. You cannot purchase or use your own devices. Our team will provision and assign billing devices to your branches based on your subscription plan and requirements.

How Billing Devices Work

  • Contact our support team to request billing devices for your branches
  • Our team will assign approved devices to your specific branches
  • Devices are pre-configured and ready for billing operations
  • Each device is linked to a specific branch in your account
  • Transactions from devices automatically sync to your branch data (cloud-connected mode)
  • Devices can operate offline and sync when internet is available

Device Modes

Cloud-Connected Mode (VPOS Billing):

  • Devices sync data to secure cloud servers
  • Real-time inventory updates across all devices
  • Centralized reporting and analytics
  • Manager and multi-branch support
  • Access data from anywhere via Admin application

Standalone Mode (VPOS Billing Offline):

  • Devices do NOT connect to cloud servers
  • All data stays on your local devices and network
  • Devices communicate with each other via local network
  • No manager accounts (branch-level only)
  • You are responsible for data backup and security
  • Ideal for businesses preferring complete data privacy

Device Responsibilities

  • The software on billing devices is licensed, not sold
  • Do not modify, reverse-engineer, or redistribute the device software
  • You are responsible for physical device damage, loss, or malfunction
  • We provide software support and updates only
  • Device repairs or replacements may incur additional charges

🗑️ Account Deactivation & Data Retention

Subscription Expiry

  • When your subscription expires, some features may be restricted
  • You can still access billing devices in offline mode
  • Cloud data access may be limited until subscription renewal
  • Renew your subscription to restore full access

Account Deactivation

If your account is deactivated, all your data is retained for 90 days but you cannot access the system. Contact our support team at support@vposindia.com to reactivate.

Account Deletion

If your account is scheduled for deletion:

  • 15-day grace period: Your account remains active with all data
  • During this time, you can contact support to cancel the deletion
  • After 15 days: The account and data are permanently deleted with the following processing:
What Happens During Account Deletion:
  • Customer Records: All customer contact information (names, phone numbers) is permanently deleted
  • Transaction Records: Bills and invoices are anonymized (customer names replaced with "[REDACTED]") and retained for 6 years minimum per Indian tax law (GST Act 2017, Income Tax Act 1961)
  • Business Data: Product inventory, pricing history, and business configuration are permanently deleted
  • Manager Accounts: All manager accounts associated with your business are permanently deleted
  • Billing Devices: All devices are deactivated and reclaimed by Value Tech Solutions
  • Local Device Data: Standalone mode devices retain local data (not deleted by us — you must manually clear it)

Tax Compliance Note: Anonymized transaction records are retained for minimum 6 years from the end of the financial year (April 1 - March 31) as required by Indian GST Act 2017 Section 36 and Income Tax Act 1961 Section 44AA. This ensures we can provide necessary records for tax audits while protecting customer privacy.
